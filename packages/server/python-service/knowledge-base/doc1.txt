Frequently Asked Questions:
Q: How often should I replace the filter?
A: The Nano-Carbon filter should be replaced every 6 months or after 500 liters of water, whichever comes first.
Q: Does it remove fluoride?
A: Yes, the AquaPure Mini uses a 4-stage filtration process that removes 98% of fluoride and 99.9% of bacteria.
Q: Is the pitcher dishwasher safe?
A: The outer pitcher is safe for the top rack of a dishwasher, but the filter housing and electronic lid must be hand-washed.
Q: What does the blue light mean?
A: A steady blue light indicates the water is fully purified and ready to drink.
Q: Is it suitable for humid countries?
A: Yes, but humidity should remain between 40-70% for optimal performance. In hot summer months like July, keep the unit in a shaded indoor area to avoid condensation.
