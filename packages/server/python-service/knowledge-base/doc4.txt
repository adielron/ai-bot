Operating Instructions:
1. Water Tank: The removable tank holds 1.5 liters. Always use filtered water to prevent descaling issues.
2. Brewing: Use the "Intense" setting for espresso shots and "Smooth" for Americano.
3. Maintenance: The "Clean" light will flash orange every 200 cycles or every 3 months. To clean, insert the BrewMaster cleaning tablet into the pod slot and run a long cycle without coffee.
Temperature Control: The machine maintains a constant 92°C (197.6°F) for optimal extraction.
Weather Note: Best performance is in kitchen humidity between 30-50%. In hot July weather, avoid placing the unit near direct sunlight or steam.
Troubleshooting: If the red light stays on, the pressure valve is stuck. Turn off the machine for 10 minutes.
Country Tip: In France and Australia, use the descaling mode every 6 weeks if water hardness is high.
