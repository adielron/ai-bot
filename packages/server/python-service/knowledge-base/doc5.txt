Country: Germany
Weather Summary:

- May: Pleasant spring weather 10°C to 20°C with moderate rainfall.
- August: Warm summer month with averages of 18°C to 26°C.

- Typical smartphone price range: 800-1,200 EUR.
- Monthly public transit pass: 70-100 EUR in major cities.
- Average dinner cost: 20-35 EUR.
Seasonal Advice:
